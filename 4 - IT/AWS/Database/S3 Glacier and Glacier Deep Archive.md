**Amazon S3 Glacier and Glacier Deep Archive** solve the problem of keeping data for years at the lowest possible cost when you almost never need it. Examples are compliance records, old backups, medical images, and legal archives. They're now S3 storage classes: Glacier Instant Retrieval, Glacier Flexible Retrieval, and Glacier Deep Archive. Storage is extremely cheap, and Deep Archive is the lowest-cost storage AWS offers. The trade-off is retrieval time and cost. Instant Retrieval returns data in milliseconds, Flexible Retrieval takes minutes to hours, and Deep Archive takes up to around 12 to 48 hours. Glacier classes also have minimum storage durations, so they're a poor fit for data you might delete soon. They're often the final step in an S3 lifecycle policy.
