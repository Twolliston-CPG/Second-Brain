**AWS Global Accelerator**  
Problem: traffic crossing the public internet takes unpredictable, congested routes, and clients that hardcode IPs can't easily fail over between regions. Global Accelerator gives you two static anycast IP addresses. User traffic enters AWS's private global backbone at the nearest edge and rides it to your application, which improves performance and enables fast regional failover. The key contrast with CloudFront is that CloudFront caches content, while Global Accelerator doesn't cache anything. It just gives you a better network path, and it works for non-HTTP traffic like TCP and UDP, which suits gaming, IoT, and VoIP.
