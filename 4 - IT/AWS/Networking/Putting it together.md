**How they fit together in a typical setup:** a user types your domain, and **Route 53** resolves it. Static assets come from **CloudFront**. Dynamic requests hit an **ALB** in a public subnet of your **VPC**, which forwards them to app servers in private subnets guarded by **Security Groups**. Your corporate office reaches the same VPC privately via **Direct Connect**, and **Transit Gateway** ties that VPC to the company's other VPCs.
