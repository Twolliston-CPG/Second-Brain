**Amazon DynamoDB** solves the problem of needing extremely fast, predictable performance at massive scale. It's a NoSQL key-value and document store: you look items up by key and get single-digit-millisecond responses whether you have a thousand users or a hundred million. It's serverless and scales automatically. The trade-off is that you give up flexible SQL queries and joins, so you design your tables around known access patterns. It's typical for shopping carts, user sessions, gaming leaderboards, and IoT data.
