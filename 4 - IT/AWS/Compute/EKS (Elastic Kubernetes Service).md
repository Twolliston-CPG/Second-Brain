The problem: Kubernetes has become the industry standard for container orchestration, but running its control plane yourself is hard, since you have to keep it highly available, upgraded, and secure. EKS runs the Kubernetes control plane for you. You get standard, upstream Kubernetes, so your skills, tools (Helm, kubectl), and workloads stay portable across clouds and on-premises. It's for teams already invested in Kubernetes or needing multi-cloud portability, and it costs more operational complexity than ECS.
